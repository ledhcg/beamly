# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ledhcg/beamly/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an issue with the bug report form. Check [Troubleshooting](README.md#troubleshooting) first.
- **Security issues:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never post them publicly.

Beamly is maintained by volunteers in their spare time, so responses may take a few days.
